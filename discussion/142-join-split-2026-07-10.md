# 142 · MoleculeAst join / split — disjoint union and components

Status: Active (design discussion)
Date: 2026-07-10
Relates: 086 (origin design — edit-API evolution), 141 (L3 `Fragment` consumer of `join`), reactions
(bimolecular bracket: `join` two reactants, react, `split` the products), 042 (relational core).

## Purpose

`join` = put two `MoleculeAst`s into one by **disjoint union**. `split` = decompose a `MoleculeAst`
into its **connected components**. Both were designed in 086 and are unimplemented. `join` unblocks L3
fragments (`attach`/`+`) and bimolecular reactions; `split` is its logical inverse (the two bracket a
reaction). This detour builds both as one batch, since they share the id-offset / correspondence
machinery, then L3 resumes on top.

## Settled already (prior discussion, 2026-07-09)

- **`join` is pure disjoint concatenation**, returning a `MoleculeCorrespondence`. **Not** a
  pushout/gluing (that is the reaction path). No chemistry — it only renumbers and concatenates.
- **`split` is conservative**: union-find over **all** relations — any relation (bond, dative,
  aromatic, multicenter, noncovalent, stereo site+ligands) binds its atoms into one component. Nothing
  is dropped and nothing errors; an overlay spanning otherwise-disconnected atoms simply keeps them in
  **one** component (it prevents the split rather than being refused). Returns a `Vec` of
  `(MoleculeAst, MoleculeCorrespondence)`.
- **`IdRemapping` → retire in favour of `MoleculeCorrespondence`** (de-TIMTOWTDI). The graph-level
  dense `Remapping` (and `Correspondence`) in graph-core **stay** — they are the lighter graph-scoped
  primitive; only the ast-level `IdRemapping` is redundant with `MoleculeCorrespondence`.

## Code inventory (what we build on)

- `MoleculeAst::from_parts(atoms, bonds, dative, aromatic, multicenter, noncovalent, stereo_atoms,
  stereo_bonds, constraints)` — full constructor.
- `MoleculeEditor` `add_*` for every family + `constraints_mut()`.
- Read views for every family (`atoms()`, `bonds().atom_ids()`, `dative_bonds()`, …,
  `stereo_atoms()` with `.site_id()`/`.ligands()`, `stereo_bonds()`).
- `MoleculeCorrespondence` = eight per-family `Correspondence<Id>`; `new(8×)` and
  `induce(lhs, rhs, atoms: Correspondence<NodeId>)` (derives bonds + every overlay from just the atom
  mapping).
- `Correspondence<Id>` = `Vec<(Id, Id)>` mates (graph-core).
- graph-core `Remapping { nodes: Vec<NodeId>, edges: Vec<EdgeId> }` with `map_node`/`map_edge`;
  `StereoLigand::remap(&Remapping)` (**private** today — visibility change needed).
- ast `IdRemapping` (eight `HashMap`s); `Constraints::remap(&IdRemapping)`,
  `Constraint::remap(&IdRemapping)` (slated for retirement above).
- graph-core `ConnectedComponentsAlgorithm` and `UnionFind` — the component machinery for `split`.

## The remap-abstraction problem (the crux)

Three id-mapping types are in play, and `join`/`split` must remap entities with one of them:

| type | shape | scope | used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| graph-core `Remapping` | dense `Vec<NodeId>`, `Vec<EdgeId>` | graph (nodes/edges) | `StereoLigand::remap`, graph ops |
| ast `IdRemapping` | 8 `HashMap<XId, XId>` | all families | `Constraints`/`Constraint::remap` |
| ast `MoleculeCorrespondence` | 8 `Correspondence<XId>` (mates) | all families | `join`/`split` return |

The retirement decision says `MoleculeCorrespondence` subsumes `IdRemapping`. So the open work is: make
`Constraints`/`Constraint::remap` consume a `MoleculeCorrespondence` (or a cheap lookup projected from
it) and delete `IdRemapping`. Stereo ligands remap at the **graph** level, so they keep using the dense
`Remapping`, which `join` builds from its atom/bond offset (a `MoleculeCorrespondence` → `Remapping`
projection for the node/edge factors). Net: one ast-level mapping type (`MoleculeCorrespondence`) plus
the graph-level `Remapping` it projects to for ligands.

## `join` — design

Signature (**D-sig**): `pub fn join(&self, other: &MoleculeAst) -> (MoleculeAst, MoleculeCorrespondence)`.
`self` is the prefix (its ids unchanged); `other`'s ids are offset per family; the correspondence maps
`other` → the union. (Alternative: the 086 phrasing `&mut self -> MoleculeCorrespondence` — append in
place. The `&self`-returning-body form is friendlier to the functional fragment algebra.)

Body construction: `self.clone().edit()`, then append `other`'s entities family-by-family. Per-family
offset = `self`'s count in that family. Participant remapping:

- atoms — none (sequential new ids); bonds/dative/aromatic/multicenter/noncovalent — atom (and bond)
  participants shifted by the integer offset.
- stereo_atoms / stereo_bonds — site shifted; ligands via `StereoLigand::remap(&Remapping)` where the
  `Remapping` is `other`'s node/edge offset.
- molecule constraints — `other`'s constraints remapped by the retirement-era mapping and merged.

Correspondence: built directly from the per-family offsets (exact, O(n)) — `mates[k] = (other_id_k,
union_id_{offset+k})`. (`induce` is available but unnecessary here since the mapping is a known offset.)
Coverage: all eight families + molecule constraints.

## `split` — design

Signature: `pub fn split(&self) -> Vec<(MoleculeAst, MoleculeCorrespondence)>` — each component with a
correspondence relating its compact ids to the original.

Components: a `UnionFind` over the atoms, unioning the atom set of **every** relation (bonds via the
graph's own components, then dative/aromatic/multicenter/noncovalent/stereo participants — conservative,
so a spanning overlay merges components). Per component: gather its atoms + the relations all of whose
participants fall inside it, compact-renumber, `from_parts`. Molecule-level constraints go to the
component holding their atoms (conservative union-find guarantees a constraint's atoms share one
component). Component order: by lowest original atom id (deterministic).

## Decisions (settled 2026-07-10)

- **D-sig** — `join_from(&mut self, other: &MoleculeAst) -> MoleculeCorrespondence` is the primitive
  (append `other` in place); `join(&self, other) -> (MoleculeAst, MoleculeCorrespondence)` = clone then
  `join_from`.
- **D-remap** — **keep `IdRemapping` (and `IdCompaction`)**. The retirement into `MoleculeCorrespondence`
  is a *separate future slice*: `IdRemapping` is load-bearing across the reaction hot path
  (`delta::remap_delta`, `reaction_span`, `reaction`, `molecule/pushout`) plus all ~9 per-family
  constraint `remap` methods, and the retirement carries a real hot-path perf question (per-application
  8-HashMap construction vs sorted-vec + O(log n) lookup — plausibly a win, but must be measured).
  join/split don't need it: `join` remaps by offset arithmetic and reuses the existing
  `Constraints::remap` via a throwaway offset `IdRemapping`; `split` remaps by a local construction map.
  The `Correspondence` HashMap-cache worry is moot regardless — `right_of` is a binary search and
  `is_total()` already models total relabels. The `reversed_remapping` HashMap in `reaction_span.rs`
  folds into that later cleanup.
- **D-corr-dir** — `join` correspondence maps `other → union`; `split` maps each `component → original`;
  `reverse()` (exists) covers the inverse.
- **D-split-constraints** — molecule-level constraints are **conservative binders** in the same
  union-find as structural relations: a constraint keeps the components its referenced entities fall in
  together. The splitter never judges whether a constraint is "strong enough" — a constraint spanning
  genuinely disconnected parts just prevents the split; deciding if that is an *inconsistency* is a
  validator's job (see follow-on). The `Constraint` walker collects referenced **entities** (one per
  `remap` arm, stopping at the entity — not atoms) via the entity→component splitting map `split`
  already builds; atoms re-enter only as a component representative to perform the union.
- **D-stereo-vis** — `StereoLigand::remap` → `pub(crate)`.
- **D-loc** — inherent methods on `MoleculeAst`.
- **D-corr-build** — build the `join` correspondence directly from the per-family offsets (exact, O(n));
  `induce` not needed.

## Impl plan

- **S0 — dropped.** `StereoLigand { atom_id, kind }` has public fields, so `join_from` offsets ligands
  by arithmetic (`StereoLigand::new(shift(atom_id), kind)`) — no dense `Remapping`, no visibility change.
- **S1 — done** (`molecule.rs`). `join_from(&mut self, other) -> MoleculeCorrespondence` appends
  `other`'s eight families (participants offset by `self`'s per-family counts; stereo via direct
  relation-set iteration `relation_ids`/`participants_1`/`participants_2`/`data`) plus molecule
  constraints (per-`Constraint::remap` with a throwaway offset `IdRemapping`). `join(&self, other)` =
  clone + `join_from`; the correspondence is built directly from the offsets (`offset_correspondence`
  over `Correspondence::from_images`). Five tests: atoms/bonds + correspondence, in-place `join_from`,
  an overlay, a stereo entity, a molecule constraint.
- **S2a — done** (`molecule.rs`). Structural `split`: `UnionFind` over bonds + all four atom-only
  overlays (via `union_participants`); components ordered by lowest atom; each component built through a
  fresh editor with compacted ids; correspondence via `MoleculeCorrespondence::induce(component, self,
  atom_correspondence)` (derives every family from the atom map — no per-family bookkeeping). Guards
  stereo + molecule-constraints for now. Three tests: two-component split, an aromatic system binding
  two otherwise-disconnected bonds into one component, `split`∘`join` roundtrip recovering the parts.
- **S2b (stereo) — done** (`molecule.rs`). Stereo in `split`: union pass (site + ligand atoms;
  `stereo_bond` site resolved to its bond's endpoints) and per-component extraction — ligands offset via
  `ligand.remap(&compaction)` (a `Remapping` from the atom compaction), the `stereo_bond` site mapped
  through a per-component `bond_compact` map built as bonds are added. Guard narrowed to constraints
  only. Test: a stereo atom binding site + ligands into one component, separate from a lone bond.
- **S2b (constraints) — done** (`molecule.rs`). `constraint_atoms` / `relational_constraint_atoms`
  walk every `Constraint` arm (per-entity id → read accessor; `MoleculeConstraint` with `None` = whole
  molecule; all `RelationalConstraint` variants = primary entity's atoms + explicit operands;
  `And`/`Or`/`Not` recurse). Union pass unions each constraint's atoms (conservative binder); extraction
  routes each constraint to its component and remaps to compact ids via `constraint.remap(&IdRemapping)`
  (`idremapping_from_correspondence` reverses the component's `induce` correspondence). Guard removed.
  Two tests: a `ChargeSum` binding two structural components into one; a constraint routed to its
  component and remapped.
- **S3 — connectivity validator — done** (`ast/validate/connectivity.rs`). Unlike `split` (mechanical),
  a validator consults a **model** — the user's view of "connected". `ConnectivityModel` (8 bools;
  defaults: `allow_disconnected` true, dative/multicenter/noncovalent/constraints true,
  aromatic/stereo_atom/stereo_bond false). Semantics: connectivity = the **localized-bond** graph's
  components; `allow_disconnected=false` ⇒ molecule must be one component; each
  `allow_disconnected_<family>=false` ⇒ every relation of that family (constraint atoms via the now-`pub`
  `constraint_atoms`) must keep its atoms in one bond component. `ConnectivityValidator::new(&model)
  .validate(ast) -> Result<Solution<(), ConnectivityContradiction>, ConnectivityError>`, first
  contradiction (mirrors the entity validator). Five tests. Re-exported at `ast::`.

Deferred (separate slice): the `IdRemapping → MoleculeCorrespondence` retirement + `reversed_remapping`
cleanup.
